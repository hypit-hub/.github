[Hypit](https://github.com/hypit-ai/hypit) is an open-source video engine that renders in headless
Chromium, driven from a CLI with your own API keys. Powerful, and out of reach for most creators.
**Hypit Hub** is what the community builds around it: the structure that already earned the views,
ready to fork, plus the boring middle automated.

## What we're building

| | What | Where | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🎬 **Templates** | SVML presets you read in place: full script, storyboard, rewriteable prompts, delivery checklist. Swap the product, keep the structure. | [hypit.si/templates](https://hypit.si/templates) | filling in |
| 🔁 **Workflows** | n8n pipelines that watch a niche, cut remakes with the engine, and queue the uploads. | — | 🚧 planned |
| ☁️ **Cloud** | The same engine wrapped in a browser: pick a template, fill in the product, get the video. No terminal, no Node. | — | 🚧 in the works |
| 🌐 **Website** | Landing page, template library, waitlist: [hypit.si](https://hypit.si). | this org | ✅ live |

## Get involved

- **Join the waitlist** at [hypit.si](https://hypit.si): one email at beta launch, no newsletter. The waitlist sets the order when cloud seats open.
- **Watch the repos**: everything ships in the open.
- **Build in public**: progress, pitfalls and renders get posted as they happen.
